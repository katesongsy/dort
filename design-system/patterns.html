03 / Flows

Patterns

Keep browsing reversible. Put the effects in a review the operator can cancel before approval.

On this page

Browse and inspect Blocked action Review and confirm Receipt Empty states

Browse and inspect

Wide
Show the selected record next to the queue. Tab moves focus between panes and leaves the selected row and filter in place. See the wide workspace.
Compact
Open the selected record in its own view. Esc returns to the same queue row and filter, with focus back in the queue.

Use compact mode when the terminal lacks cells for two readable panes. Keep the same task order and action labels in both layouts.

Blocked action

Say what prevents the action and what to do next. For example, name the missing checksum and direct the operator to capture a verified snapshot. Do not offer review or approval for a blocked action. Neither Enter nor y applies it.

Review and confirm

  1. Use Enter on Review restore, or an equivalent named action, to open the review. Opening it changes nothing.
  2. Use the full terminal width. Name the action, its target, what changes, and what stays untouched. Include any backup conditions or warnings. Let effects scroll above the action area.
  3. Reserve y for the explicit confirmation shown only in a ready review. Enter does not approve. Offer Esc to return without applying the action.

Receipt

After approval, replace the review with the outcome. On success, show Finished, the action taken, and what changed. If the action fails, name the failure and show a next step. Offer a way back. Repeated y or Enter must not apply the effect again.

Empty states

Distinguish an empty queue from a filter with no matches. For no matches, keep the query visible and offer to clear it. For an empty queue, explain why nothing is listed and how records appear. In both cases, clear stale details and hide record actions.
