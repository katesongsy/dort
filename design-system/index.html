Dort TUI design

Browse a queue, inspect a record, then review what will change before you approve it.

Status: Target design for new TUI features and future migration. The examples come from a standalone Rust reference, not the current Dort TUI.

Interaction standard

  1. Keep the selected row visible.
  2. Put the task before its provenance.
  3. Reserve approval for a ready review.
01 / RolesFoundationsText roles, color, and terminal cells. 02 / PartsComponentsThe queue, detail reader, and action area. 03 / FlowsPatternsBrowse and inspect, review, confirm, and recover.

The wide workspace

The queue and detail reader share the screen. Selection names the record. Focus names the pane that gets the next key.
