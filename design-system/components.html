02 / Parts

Components

Keep the queue, detail reader, and action area distinct. Name the pane that receives keys.

On this page

Workspace Queue and filter Detail reader Action area

Workspace

The wide workspace places the queue and detail reader side by side above a shared action area. In compact mode, show one pane at a time and retain the shared action area.

Selection
The record the detail reader shows. Mark its row even when the queue loses focus.
Focus
The pane that receives the next key. Name it in text. In the wide workspace, Tab changes focus without changing the selection or filter.

See the wide workspace or compare the wide and compact flows.

Queue and filter

Show a recognizable name and a short state on each row. Use ↑ and ↓ to move the selection when the queue has focus. Keep the selected row in view as the list scrolls.

Bind / to filter entry and show the active query. Keep the selection if the row still matches. If no rows match, explain the empty list and offer a way to clear the filter.

Detail reader

Put the task first. Name the record, then give its status and the facts needed to decide what to do. Put optional provenance behind a labeled disclosure such as Space Show origin. Opening the disclosure keeps the same record in view.

Let the reader scroll independently. The queue selection and action area stay put while the reader scrolls.

Action area

Use one location below the work for the current keys, the primary action, and the outcome status. Keep it visible while a queue, detail reader, or review scrolls.

Name the action, such as Review restore. If the action is blocked, replace it with a reason and next step. Show only the keys that work in the current view.

See review and confirmation.
